# Foreign Transaction Fee & Exchange Rate Policy

## Overview
Transactions processed outside the United Kingdom or in non-GBP currencies are subject to international exchange fees and Visa/Mastercard wholesale exchange rates.

## Fee Schedule
- **Non-GBP Currency Exchange Fee**: 2.75% of the total transaction amount.
- **ATM Cash Withdrawal Abroad**: 2.75% exchange fee + £1.50 international ATM processing fee.
- **Weekend Exchange Rate Markup**: 0.5% markup applied to foreign exchange conversions executed between Friday 22:00 UTC and Sunday 22:00 UTC.

## Recommendations
To avoid foreign exchange fees, consider utilizing dedicated travel accounts or multi-currency wallets when traveling abroad.
