# Open Banking Consent & 90-Day Renewal Policy

## Overview
Under FCA & PSD2 regulations, third-party AISP (Account Information Service Provider) access to your bank accounts is governed by strict 90-day consent agreements.

## Consent Lifecycle
- **Initial Authorization**: User grants explicit consent via Bank Strong Customer Authentication (SCA).
- **90-Day Expiry**: Consent automatically expires after 90 days. The AI Financial Guardian alerts users 14 days prior to expiration.
- **Revocation & Kill-Switch**: Users can revoke AISP access at any time via the Customer Profile page Emergency Kill-Switch.
