# Open Banking Customer FAQ

## How secure is Open Banking?
Open Banking uses bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption and OAuth2 OpenID Connect security standards. Applications never store or see your bank login passwords.

## Can I revoke app access?
Yes. Consent can be instantly revoked at any time via the Consent Scope settings menu in the app or through your bank's portal.

## What is standard consent duration?
Under UK FCA regulation, active consent remains valid for up to 90 days before requiring re-authentication.
