# Transaction Dispute & Fraud Guide

## What happens if I notice an unauthorized charge?
1. Immediately flag the transaction in the AI Financial Guardian app using `flag_transaction`.
2. The AI Guardian places a temporary security hold on linked payment permissions.
3. A formal dispute request is generated and submitted under UK Payment Services Regulations.

## What is the timeframe for filing a charge dispute?
Unauthorised payment claims must be reported within 13 months of the transaction debit date.
